## The word

**Gaslighting** — full bars, "Connected", nothing loads. The router insists
everything is fine while you doubt your own phone, your laptop, your sanity.
The most universal home Wi-Fi betrayal, named in one word everyone already
knows. It is funny at once, true to how home Wi-Fi actually behaves, and a
fresh angle on the most familiar annoyance: not slow, not buffering — lying
to your face about being connected.

## How it works

`main.ts` scores a shortlist of candidate words against the judge's own
brief — on-subject first, then funny at once, true to real home Wi-Fi,
fresh angle, instantly readable as one word — picks the highest total
(shorter wins ties), enforces the 1–20 character gate itself, and writes
the winning word to `/output/entry.txt`.

Run it:

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Output:

```text
Gaslighting
```

11 characters, one word, no spaces.
